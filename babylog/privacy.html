Feed & Diaper Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-19
Contact: ss19915@126.com

Feed & Diaper (Simplified Chinese: 喂奶与纸尿裤; Traditional Chinese: 餵奶與紙尿褲; the “App”) is an iPhone app for logging feeds and diaper changes, viewing a timeline, and seeing time since the last feed. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles information related to you.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account or sign-in, and does not upload your app data to our servers or to third-party business cloud services.

On your iPhone, the App stores the following content that you create:

  • Feed records (time, type, and other details you enter)
  • Diaper records (time, type, and other details you enter)

Except as described under “System Capabilities” and “Purchases” below, we do not collect personally identifiable information for advertising or profiling.

The App has no ads, no tracking SDKs, and no third-party analytics.

2. Where Data Is Stored

All feed and diaper records are stored locally on your iPhone (SwiftData) and are not synced to our servers. This version does not offer iCloud sync.

Logging entries works on-device; recording itself can be done offline.

3. System Capabilities & Permissions

Siri & Shortcuts

The App supports creating or querying records via Siri / Shortcuts (system App Intents). Speech and commands are handled by iOS; we do not operate a voice cloud and do not send your app data to our own servers.

Network Use

Network access may be used in the following cases, which are unrelated to your feed/diaper app data:

  • Checking for or downloading App updates from the App Store
  • In-app purchase, restore, and receipt validation (handled by Apple)
  • Siri / Shortcuts and other system features (whether they use the network is up to the system)

In Airplane Mode, core features (viewing and editing local records, timeline, time since last feed, and so on) still work; purchase/restore and features that depend on system networking may be limited.

4. Purchases & Trial

The App is free to download. Full ability to create new records is provided via Apple In-App Purchase:

  • 7-day trial (free during the trial)
  • One-time unlock

After the trial ends, a soft lock applies: you can still view and edit existing history, but you cannot create new records until you unlock.

Purchase and restore are handled by Apple. We do not upload your feed/diaper app data to our servers because of a purchase.

5. Retention & Deletion

  • Data is deleted when you uninstall the App (unless retained via device migration, system backup, or similar).
  • This version has no cloud account of ours; if you uninstall the App or lose the device without a system backup, data may be unrecoverable.

6. Third-Party Services

The App’s core features do not depend on third-party cloud or analytics services that would upload your app data.

In-app purchases are provided by Apple; Siri / Shortcuts are provided by iOS. We are not responsible for Apple’s or the system’s own policies; please also review Apple’s terms where applicable.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; app data is created and managed locally by the device owner. Records may relate to infant care but are entered on-device by an adult user; we do not collect children’s personal information onto our servers on that basis.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and revise the “Effective date” on this page. For material changes, we may note them in the App’s About screen or App Store release notes.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy: ss19915@126.com.
